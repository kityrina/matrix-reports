The Undercurrent

Underneath the World, there's a second pull — the 5 (The Hierophant). This is the energy of tradition, mentorship, and mastery through the lineage. While the year arcanum brings global reach and completion, the Hierophant asks you to slow down and deepen — to become *known* not just for breadth, but for the specific wisdom you've earned the right to teach.

You'll feel this most sharply in moments when the World pushes you toward expansion and the Hierophant whispers *not yet.* Someone asks you to mentor them. An institution wants your name on something. You're suddenly aware that what you know has value beyond your own projects — that it needs to be passed forward, structured, made repeatable. The Hierophant doesn't let you fake expertise. It demands you've actually walked the path before you explain it to anyone else.

Where they compound: both want mastery. The World says *complete the cycle*; the Hierophant says *now teach what that cycle taught you.* Together, they position you as someone who's not just successful, but authoritative. The World gets you the platform. The Hierophant makes sure you have something worth saying once you're on it.

Where they pull different directions: the World loves speed and integration. The Hierophant is slow, formal, almost academic. You'll feel torn between closing deals fast and going deep enough to earn real respect.

Day-to-day, the World dominates — projects finishing, doors opening, momentum. The Hierophant surfaces when someone treats you like a teacher, or when you realise the thing you built needs a structure others can learn from.
