3. **Weekly equality audit.** Every Sunday, write down three moments this week when you felt superior to someone. Name the situation, the person, the thought. Then ask: what was I protecting by staying above? What would have opened if I'd come down to their level? This is shadow work for The Overseer — making the judgment conscious so you can choose differently.
