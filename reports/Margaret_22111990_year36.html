Health & Body

Margaret, this is not a year to stay in one place physically. The Fool demands movement — literal, constant, chaotic movement — and your body will crave it. Think: spontaneous weekend trips, last-minute hikes, dancing until 3am, yoga retreats you book on a whim. The risk? Scattered energy that never lands. The Fool burns hot and bright, then crashes hard. You'll feel invincible one week, then wake up exhausted the next because you forgot to eat or sleep like a normal human. Watch the nervous system — the Fool year overstimulates. Adrenal fatigue is real here. You might notice jittery energy, trouble winding down, or that wired-but-tired feeling that makes you reach for another coffee when what you need is a nap. The antidote is boring: regular sleep, grounding practices, saying no to one out of every three invitations. Your body will also want to experiment this year — new diets, new workouts, maybe something unconventional like aerial silks or cold plunges. Go for it, but don't abandon what works in the name of novelty. The Fool loves a fresh start but doesn't always finish. If you notice unusual fatigue, heart palpitations, or persistent anxiety, see a doctor — don't write it off as stress. This arcanum tests whether you can hold freedom without burning out. The answer is yes, but only if you remember you're mortal.

Work & Money

The Fool is the arcanum of beginnings, and beginnings require endings. You'll be flooded with ideas. Some are gold. Most are distractions. Your 19 (The Sun) money channel wants you to lead, organise, and build something public-facing and financially sound. The Fool year says: do it differently this time. No corporate ladder. People will think you're reckless. You're not. You're just done pretending the safe path excites you. The trap? Starting ten things and finishing none. The Fool is a terrible project manager. Your 15 (The Devil) on the money line says you're capable of high-stakes moves and big gains, but only if you don't let fear — or ego — make the call. Don't burn bridges. Don't ghost clients. Don't assume the universe will catch you if you jump without a parachute. Freedom, yes. Stupidity, no.

Love & Relationships

The Fool year in love is exhilarating and exhausting, often at the same time. The question is: can you stay open to someone still becoming? If you're already partnered, this year will either inject new life into the relationship or expose what's already dead. The Fool doesn't do stagnation. You'll want adventure, novelty, spontaneity — and if your partner can't match that energy, you'll feel suffocated. The danger is mistaking boredom for incompatibility. Sometimes the relationship is fine; you're just restless. Before you blow it up, ask: is this actually over, or do I just need to take a solo trip? That said, if the relationship has been limping along on obligation, the Fool will hand you the exit. You won't plan it. It'll just happen — a conversation that goes sideways, a realisation you can't un-know. Trust it. This arcanum doesn't lie about love. It just refuses to fake it.

Restlessness for its own sake. The Fool's recklessness shows up as ghosting — on people, on promises, on your own word. You might justify it as "staying open," but the people around you will call it flakiness, and they won't be wrong. The other tell: you'll keep running from anything that asks you to grow roots.
