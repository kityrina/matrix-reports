The Undercurrent

16 (The Tower)

Underneath the Star, there's a second pull — the 16 (The Tower). This is the energy of sudden collapse, forced truth, structures cracking open when they've outlived their use. While the year's main current asks you to shine and make yourself visible, the undercurrent is tearing down whatever's been propping up the old version of you.

This shows up as uncomfortable revelations mid-visibility moment. You'll be putting yourself out there — new project, public platform, creative work catching eyes — and suddenly something in your personal infrastructure cracks. Could be a relationship that can't hold the weight of who you're becoming. Could be a belief about yourself that shatters the moment you step into real exposure. Could be a living situation, a business partnership, a carefully maintained image that just… breaks. The Star wants expansion; the Tower clears the decks so that expansion has room to breathe. They're not opposites — they're partners. One builds the stage, the other kicks out the rotting supports underneath it.

You'll feel the Star in your day-to-day momentum — meetings, offers, people saying yes, the light on you feels good. You'll feel the Tower in sudden ruptures you didn't schedule. A conversation that ends something. A truth you can't un-know. A structure collapsing faster than you can manage it gracefully. The Star is your public arc this year. The Tower is the private demolition that makes the arc possible. Let it fall.

Shadow Track

The shadow year dims the light before anyone can judge it. Shadow behaviours: overpolishing everything to the point nothing goes live, saying no to opportunities because you're "not ready," and flatlining emotionally when you're not the centre of attention—then resenting the people who are. You might burn energy chasing validation in the wrong rooms while ignoring the quiet work that actually inspires you. False hope looks like waiting for someone to discover you instead of putting the work where people can find it. By next birthday, you'll still be "almost ready"—and bitter about it.

Kavya, this is the year you stop dimming yourself and start building visibility — not for applause, but because The Star demands you be SEEN doing the work that matters. Your 6 (The Lovers) in money says choose WHICH stage you're stepping onto, then commit. This year isn't about going viral — it's about being REAL, out loud, where it counts. Shine with intent, or don't shine at all.
