New Strategy

The better pattern is **structured disruption** — rebellion with a plan, fire with a container. 1. **Mentor Audit.** Sit down this week and list every teacher, boss, or authority figure you've rejected or resisted in the past three years. 2. **The Sacred No.** Pick one area of your life where you've been saying no on autopilot — a career path, a family expectation, a traditional milestone — and write out *why* you're refusing it. If the reason is solid and yours, keep the no. If it's reflex rebellion because someone else wanted it for you, reconsider. Practice the difference between authentic resistance and inherited defiance.

3. **Contained Fire Practice.** Choose one small, low-stakes rule or routine you've been resisting (a daily habit, a work protocol, a family tradition) and follow it *perfectly* for two weeks. Not forever — just long enough to feel what it's like to work *with* structure instead of against it. Notice if your rebellious energy finds a different outlet when it's not bleeding into everything. The Wheel teaches mastery of timing; sometimes the revolution is knowing when to sit still.
